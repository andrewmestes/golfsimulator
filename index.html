Before you ask

The questions we get every week.

Will it fit in my space?

It wants 15 by 12 feet on the floor and about 10 feet of height. Nine feet still works — we run irons and wedges instead of drivers, which is what most guests want anyway. Below that we set up outside.

What do you need from us?

One standard 110V outlet within about fifty feet, and the footprint above. The blower runs continuously to keep the frame up, so the outlet has to stay live. That is genuinely it — we bring everything else.

Indoors or outdoors — does it matter?

Not much. Inside we set it on any flat floor; nothing anchors down and nothing marks the surface. Outside we stake into grass or use water weights on pavement. It shrugs off a breeze and light rain. Sustained high wind is the one condition we will call, and we reschedule at no charge.

How many people can actually play?

On a party rotation, roughly fifty guests an hour cycle through — everyone hits three or four shots and the leaderboard keeps them honest. For a smaller group we'll run full nine-hole rounds instead.

Do we need to bring clubs?

Never. We bring right- and left-handed sets, balls, tees and gloves in a few sizes. Anyone who wants to use their own is welcome to.

Nobody at our event golfs. Is this a bad idea?

It's usually the better idea. Beginners get real numbers and instant feedback from a host standing right there, and the games we run — closest to the pin, longest drive, target range — don't require a swing anyone's proud of.

Can you put our branding on it?

Yes. Your logo sits on the simulator screen between shots, on the leaderboard, and on the printed scorecards. Sponsors like this, which is why fundraisers often cover the rental before doors open.

How far ahead should we book?

Holiday party season and summer Saturdays go first — six to eight weeks out is safe for those. Midweek dates are usually open with two weeks' notice.

What does it cost to hold a date?

A deposit of 25% holds it, with the balance due the week of the event. Move a date for weather or venue trouble at no charge, once.
