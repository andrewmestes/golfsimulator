Before you ask

The nine questions
we get every week.

What do you need from us?

One standard 110V outlet within about fifty feet, and the footprint listed above. That's genuinely it for The Bay. The Rig brings its own power and needs nothing but a level place to park.

Our ceiling is only nine feet. Are we out?

No — you're out of drivers, not out of golf. At nine feet we run irons, wedges, closest-to-the-pin and par-3 courses, which is what most guests want anyway. Under about eight and a half feet we'd bring The Rig and set up outside instead.

How many people can actually play?

On a party rotation, roughly fifty guests an hour cycle through — everyone hits three or four shots, the leaderboard keeps them honest, and nobody waits long. For a smaller group we'll run full nine-hole rounds instead.

Do we need to bring clubs?

Never. We bring right- and left-handed sets, balls, tees, and gloves in a few sizes. Anyone who wants to use their own is welcome to.

Nobody at our event golfs. Is this a bad idea?

It's usually the better idea. Beginners get real numbers and instant feedback from a host standing right there, and the games we run — closest to the pin, longest drive, target range — don't require a swing anyone's proud of.

Can you put our branding on it?

Yes. Your logo sits on the simulator screen between shots, on the leaderboard, and on the printed scorecards. Sponsors love this, which is why fundraisers often clear the rental cost before doors open.

What happens if it rains or snows?

Indoors, nothing. Outdoors, The Rig is enclosed and climate-controlled — a February booking is genuinely one of the better ones, since it's the only golf anyone in Colorado is playing that week.

How far ahead should we book?

November through February fills first — holiday parties book that window six to eight weeks out. Summer Saturdays go early for weddings. Midweek dates are usually open with two weeks' notice.

What does it cost to hold a date?

A deposit of 25% holds it, with the balance due the week of the event. Move a date for weather or venue trouble at no charge, once.
